# Product page roadmap (target: about 100 product pages)

Rules for every page on this list: one distinct search intent, a primary keyword no other page owns, real buyer questions answered with facts we can stand behind, and the full ProductPage pattern in CLAUDE.md section 9. Pages that would only repeat a parent page with a different noun are not built. Their keyword stays on the parent and old URLs 301 there.

Not built at the owner's request: leather and faux leather garments (old leather URLs redirect to the jacket page).

Kept on parent pages (no separate page): polo shirts, tank tops, long sleeves (T-shirt page), sweatshirts and zip hoodies (hoodie page), sweatpants (joggers), cargo pants and chinos (pants), yoga pants (leggings), pyjamas (loungewear), board shorts and rash guards (swimwear), puffer vests (puffer jacket), cardigans and beanies (sweater and hat pages).
