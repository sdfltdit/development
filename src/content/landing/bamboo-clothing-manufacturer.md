  - q: What is the minimum order for bamboo clothing?

  - q: Is bamboo viscose the same as bamboo fabric?
    a: No. Most soft bamboo clothing is bamboo viscose, a regenerated fibre made from bamboo pulp. Fabric spun directly from bamboo fibre is rare and feels rough, so it is seldom used for clothing.
  - q: How must viscose from bamboo be labelled?
    a: In the US, the FTC requires the label to say rayon or viscose made from bamboo, not just bamboo. Our care labels follow the wording you confirm for each country.
  - q: Is bamboo clothing antibacterial?
    a: You should not claim it. The FTC says rayon made from bamboo does not keep the plant's natural antimicrobial properties. We avoid this claim and suggest you do the same.
  - q: Can I order custom bamboo clothing for babies?
    a: Yes. Bodysuits, daywear tops and leggings are common baby orders. Testing for children's clothing is arranged by you or your importer for the market you sell in.
  - q: Can private label bamboo clothing carry my own labels?
    a: Yes. Private label bamboo clothing leaves our factory with your printed neck labels, care labels and packing. Our name does not appear on it.
  - q: Does bamboo viscose shrink?
    a: It can shrink more than cotton. We wash-test the bulk fabric before cutting and adjust the pattern, so the finished garment matches your size chart.
