  - q: Do you make reversible basketball jerseys?

  - q: Which fabric is best for basketball jerseys?
    a: Polyester mesh is the usual choice for basketball jerseys because it breathes well. Dazzle has a shiny, retro look. Interlock is smoother and takes sublimation prints sharply.
  - q: Are tackle twill numbers or sublimated numbers better?
    a: Tackle twill is stitched on and gives a raised, classic look. Sublimated numbers are part of the fabric, lighter and never peel. Both are done in our factory.
