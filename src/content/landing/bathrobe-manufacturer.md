faq:
  - q: What fabric is best for hotel bathrobes?
    a: Heavy cotton terry is the classic choice for hotel bathrobes, because it absorbs water well. Waffle weave is lighter and dries faster, so many spas prefer it.
  - q: What GSM should a terry bathrobe be?
    a: It depends on the feel you want. Heavier terry bathrobes feel plush but take longer to dry. We send swatches in a few weights so you can choose by touch.
  - q: Do waffle bathrobes shrink?
    a: Cotton waffle can shrink after washing. We wash-test the fabric and add the allowance to the pattern before cutting.
  - q: Can you embroider our logo on custom bathrobes?
    a: Yes. Logos are embroidered in-house on the chest, pocket or back, and the stitch file is approved on the sample.
  - q: Do you make one-size robes?
    a: Yes. One-size robes are common for hotels. Retail brands usually choose S to XXL, and we also make kids' robes.
  - q: Can bathrobes be packed for hotels?
    a: Yes. Robes can be folded and bagged one by one, or packed in bulk by size, to your packing list.
