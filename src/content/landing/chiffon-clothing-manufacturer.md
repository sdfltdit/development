  - q: What is the difference between chiffon vs georgette?
    a: Both are sheer crepe weaves. Chiffon is lighter, smoother and more see-through. Georgette is heavier, grainier and drapes with more weight.
  - q: Polyester chiffon or silk chiffon?
    a: Polyester chiffon costs less, creases less and takes sublimation print. Silk chiffon feels softer and breathes better but costs more and needs gentler care.
  - q: How do you stop chiffon seams from pulling apart?
    a: We use French seams, a short stitch and fine needles, and add seam allowance where the fabric slips. Each lot is checked for seam slippage on the sample.
  - q: Do chiffon dresses need a lining?
    a: Most do, unless the sheer look is the point. We agree the lining, slip or second layer when the sample is planned.
  - q: Can I order chiffon blouses in my own print?
    a: Yes. We sublimation print your artwork on polyester chiffon in our own factory. Silk chiffon is printed at the mill.
