## Bangladesh's clothing industry in numbers

Ready-made garments are Bangladesh's largest export. These figures come from the Export Promotion Bureau (EPB) for the fiscal year July 2025 to June 2026, as reported by [The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1482556) on 8 July 2026:

| Market | FY2025-26 garment exports | Change on previous year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All markets | US$38.70 billion | −1.64% |
| European Union | US$19.06 billion | −3.31% |
| United States | US$7.74 billion | +2.63% |
| United Kingdom | US$4.39 billion | +0.91% |
| Canada | US$1.34 billion | +3.20% |

Two trends matter for buyers. First, North American and UK demand held up while EU demand fell. Second, prices were under pressure: Eurostat data reported by [bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/business/zbu9ocdc4g) on 16 November 2026 shows the average EU import price of Bangladeshi clothing per kilogram down 6.54% in January to September 2026. That is good news for buyers negotiating today, but it also squeezes factories, which makes checking a supplier's stability more important.

## What Bangladesh does best

- **Cotton knitwear.** T-shirts, polos, hoodies, sweatshirts and joggers are the core of the industry, and knit exports are larger than woven exports.
- **Price on basics.** Labour and knit fabric costs make cotton basics competitive at volume.
- **Trade access.** As a least developed country, Bangladesh currently enjoys duty-free or reduced-duty access to many markets, including the EU, UK, Canada, Australia and Japan. The United States does not grant this preference, so US buyers pay standard duty plus any additional tariffs in force.

## Where Bangladesh is weaker

- **Very small runs.** Many factories start at a thousand pieces or more per style. Low-MOQ factories exist, including us at {{moq}} {{moqUnit}}, but they are a minority.
- **Fabric variety.** Much synthetic and technical fabric is imported, which adds time for performance or specialty products.
- **Lead time to market.** Sea freight from {{port}} to Europe or North America takes longer than shipping from nearer suppliers such as Turkey or Mexico.

The right choice depends on your product. For a cotton T-shirt or hoodie programme, Bangladesh is hard to beat on price. For a technical jacket, China or Vietnam is usually the better fit.

## Trade changes to plan for

- **LDC graduation on 24 November 2029.** Bangladesh's least developed country status ends on that date. Markets such as the EU and UK have transition rules, so preferences do not all end on the same day. Ask your customs broker how your country handles it before signing multi-year contracts.
- **United States tariffs.** US duty on Bangladeshi clothing has changed several times since 2025. Check the current Harmonized Tariff Schedule rate for your product code before you confirm a US order.
- **EU sustainability rules.** The EU is phasing in requirements such as digital product passports for textiles. These mainly apply to the brand selling in the EU. Still, your factory will be asked for supply-chain data, so choose one that can provide it.

## How to choose a clothing manufacturer in Bangladesh

1. **Check it is a real company.** Ask for the company registration and trade association membership, and confirm the street address.
2. **See the production floor.** Visit if you can, or ask for recent photos and buyer references.
3. **Get the terms in writing.** MOQ per style, colour and size split, sample fee, lead time and payment schedule should all be on the quote.
4. **Order a paid sample first.** Judge the fit, stitching and communication before committing to bulk.
5. **Pay the company, not a person.** Only pay the company bank account named on the proforma invoice. Be wary of anyone who asks you to pay a personal account or a new account by email.
6. **Agree how quality is checked.** Ask which AQL level the factory uses and whether your own inspector can visit before shipment.
