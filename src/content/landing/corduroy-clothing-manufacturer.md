  - q: What is wide wale corduroy?

  - q: Do you make corduroy jackets with sherpa lining?

  - q: Which wale is best for corduroy trousers?
    a: Standard wale suits most corduroy trousers. Needlecord feels smarter and lighter. Wide wale gives a relaxed, retro feel.
  - q: Why does corduroy look a different shade from different angles?
    a: Corduroy has a pile that catches light. We cut every panel with the pile running the same way, so the shade looks even.
  - q: Can you garment-dye corduroy?
    a: Yes. Garment dyeing softens the cord and gives a faded colour. We plan shrinkage into the pattern first.
  - q: Does corduroy crush or lose its pile?
    a: Pile can flatten with heavy pressing. We press corduroy face down on a soft surface and never iron the pile flat.
