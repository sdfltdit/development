  - q: What is the minimum order for cotton clothing?

  - q: What is the difference between combed cotton and carded cotton?
    a: Combed cotton has the short fibres brushed out before spinning. The yarn is smoother and pills less, so it feels softer. Carded cotton keeps more short fibres, which costs less but feels rougher.
  - q: Is ring-spun cotton worth the extra cost?
    a: For tees sold at retail, usually yes. Ring-spun cotton is twisted into a finer, stronger yarn, so the fabric feels softer and takes prints well. Open-end yarn suits budget and promotional ranges.
  - q: How much does cotton shrink?
    a: Cotton jersey can shrink several per cent in length on the first wash. We wash-test the bulk fabric before cutting and adjust the pattern so the finished size matches your chart.
  - q: Which cotton fabric weight should I choose?
    a: Lighter jersey suits summer tees and layering. Midweight jersey suits everyday tees. Heavy jersey and fleece suit boxy tees, hoodies and sweatshirts. We send swatches so you can feel each cotton fabric weight.
  - q: Can I order custom cotton clothing in organic cotton?
    a: Yes, if you pick a certified organic fabric from a mill. The organic claim itself rests on that mill's paperwork, not on us, so confirm it before printing labels.
  - q: Do you make cotton blends?
    a: Yes. Polyester in the mix cuts shrinkage and fading, and a little spandex gives stretch for fitted styles. Your quote names the blend down to the percentage.
