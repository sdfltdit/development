faq:
  - q: Do you sell custom hang tags on their own?
    a: No. Our sister company prints hang tags only for clothing we make for you, from {{moq}} {{moqUnit}}.
  - q: What is the difference between hang tags and swing tags?
    a: None. Swing tags is the British name and hang tags the American one. Both mean a printed card attached to a garment with a string or fastener.
  - q: What card weight should my hang tags be?
    a: Most retail tags use 300 to 450 GSM card. Go thicker, up to about 700 GSM, or layer two cards for a rigid, premium feel.
  - q: Which hang tag strings look best?
    a: Plain cotton or waxed string suits most brands. Choose a string with a plastic seal if a retailer wants the tag locked on.
  - q: Can you print barcode tags for my retailer?
    a: Yes. Send your UPC or EAN list by style, colour and size, and we print and scan every barcode before bulk.
  - q: Do kraft hang tags cost more?
    a: Usually not by much. Kraft hang tags use uncoated brown card, which prints in darker, softer colours than white card.
  - q: Where is the hang tag attached?
    a: Usually through the care label, the neck label or a buttonhole. We never punch a fastener through the main fabric.
