faq:
  - q: What does cut and sew mean?
    a: The garment is made from fabric rolls, not from a ready-made blank. Panels are cut from your pattern and sewn together, so the fit, fabric and details are yours.
  - q: Do you offer full package production and CMT?
    a: Yes, both. With full package we buy the fabric and trims. With CMT you supply the fabric and we charge for cutting, making, trims and packing.
  - q: Can I supply my own fabric?
    a: Yes. Send it with the mill test report, and we confirm consumption and run shade and shrinkage checks before cutting.
  - q: Which garments can a cut and sew clothing manufacturer make?
    a: Almost any. We make tees, hoodies, joggers, activewear, shirts, trousers, dresses, outerwear and denim, in knit and woven fabric.
  - q: How is quality checked?
    a: On the line during sewing, at the end of each line and in a final AQL {{aql}} audit. You can also send your own inspector.
  - q: Can different styles share one minimum?
    a: No. Each style needs {{moq}} pieces, but styles in the same fabric share one fabric order, which helps cost.
