  - q: Are you a jeans manufacturer for small brands?
    a: Yes. Many of our denim buyers are new brands launching one or two fits. We pattern your fit, sample it in the denim you pick, then produce from {{moq}} pieces.
  - q: How much does denim shrink in the wash?
    a: Rigid denim can shrink a lot in length when washed. We wash test each fabric lot and size the pattern so the finished jeans match your chart after washing.
  - q: Are you a denim jacket manufacturer too?
    a: Yes. Jackets are cut from the same denim as your jeans and washed alongside them, with your own patch or embroidery.
  - q: Can private label jeans have our own buttons and rivets?
    a: Yes. Private label jeans can carry branded shank buttons, rivets, zip pullers, back patches and printed pocket bags. Branded hardware has its own minimum, set out on your quote.
  - q: Why do wash shades vary between orders?
    a: Indigo dye varies between fabric lots, so the same wash recipe can give a slightly different shade. We check each lot against your approved standard and adjust the recipe before bulk.
