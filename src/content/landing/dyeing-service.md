  - q: How is garment dyeing different from fabric dyeing?
    a: Fabric dyeing colours the cloth before cutting. Garment dyeing colours the finished piece, so seams and thread fade softly and the hand feels broken in.
  - q: What is pigment dyeing?
    a: Pigment dyeing coats the fibre surface instead of soaking in. The colour fades with each wash for a vintage look, which brands choose on purpose.
  - q: What is overdyeing?
    a: Overdyeing adds a second colour over an already dyed or printed garment. It softens prints and gives a washed, layered tone.
  - q: Can you match custom dyed clothing to my Pantone colours?
    a: Yes. We dye lab dips to your Pantone references and approve them before the sample. Garment dye always varies a little between batches.
  - q: Do garment dyed pieces shrink?
    a: Yes, more than fabric-dyed pieces. We wash-test and cut the pattern larger so the finished size matches your chart.
  - q: Can prints go on before dyeing?
    a: Yes. Prints and embroidery before dyeing take on a soft, faded look. We test the effect on the sample.
