faq:
  - q: What is the minimum order for custom embroidered patches?
    a: Custom embroidered patches start at {{moq}} pieces per design. Two designs or two sizes count as two items.
  - q: Iron-on patches or sew-on patches?
    a: Iron-on patches are quick to apply but can lift after many washes. Sew-on patches last longest. Many brands iron them in place, then sew round the edge.
  - q: What are hook-and-loop patches used for?
    a: Hook-and-loop patches let the wearer swap patches on jackets, bags, caps and uniforms. We supply the matching loop panel for the garment if you need it.
  - q: Merrow border or heat-cut edge?
    a: A merrow border is a raised, rounded edge that suits circles, squares and shields. A heat-cut edge follows any shape and gives a flatter, cleaner look.
  - q: Do you make uniform patches?
    a: Yes. Uniform patches for security, hospitality, schools and sports clubs are made in matching colours and sewn onto uniforms if we make those too.
  - q: How small can text be on a patch?
    a: Very fine text loses detail in thread. We check your artwork before the sample and suggest a larger size or a printed patch when text is too small.
  - q: What does embroidery coverage mean?
    a: Coverage is how much of the patch is stitched. At 100% coverage the whole face is thread. At lower coverage the twill base shows through, which costs less and feels thinner.
