faq:
  - q: What is the minimum order for custom embroidery?
    a: Custom embroidery is part of a garment order, which starts at {{moq}} {{moqUnit}} per style. One logo can run across every colour and size in that order.
  - q: What is 3D puff embroidery?
    a: 3D puff embroidery stitches over a foam layer, so the design stands up from the fabric. It works best on bold shapes and caps, not fine text.
  - q: Do you charge for embroidery digitising?
    a: Embroidery digitising is done in-house for each new design. Any setup cost appears on your written quote, and reorders reuse the same file.
  - q: How small can embroidered logos be?
    a: Most embroidered logos need text at least 5 mm tall to stay readable. We test small details on a sew-out before bulk.
  - q: Will embroidery pucker on thin fabric?
    a: Not when the backing and density suit the fabric. We choose both for each garment and check a sew-out first.
  - q: Can you match thread to my brand colours?
    a: Yes. We match threads to your colour references and approve them on the sew-out.
  - q: Does the backing feel rough inside?
    a: We trim backing close and can add a soft cover on tees and baby wear, so nothing scratches.
