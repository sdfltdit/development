  - q: What is brushed fleece?
    a: A knit whose back loops are brushed into a soft, warm pile. Sweatshirt fleece is brushed on the back, and polar fleece on both sides.
  - q: Cotton fleece or polar fleece fabric?
    a: Cotton fleece breathes and prints well, so it suits hoodies and sweats. Polar fleece fabric is polyester, light, warm and quick to dry, so it suits jackets and outdoor layers.
  - q: What is anti-pill fleece?
    a: Polyester fleece finished at the mill so the surface forms fewer bobbles. The pilling grade comes from the mill's test report.
  - q: What weight of fleece should I choose?
    a: Around 280 to 320 GSM suits most hoodies and sweatshirts. Go heavier for a premium feel, or lighter for layering and kidswear.
  - q: Can I get fleece in a custom colour?
    a: Yes. Your colour is matched by lab dip on the fleece, and the ribs for cuffs and hems are dyed to match.
