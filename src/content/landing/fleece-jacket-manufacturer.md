  - q: What is the difference between polar fleece jackets and microfleece?
    a: Polar fleece jackets use a thicker, lofty fleece that traps more warmth. Microfleece is thinner and lighter, so it suits layering and active wear.
  - q: Do you make sherpa jackets?
    a: Yes. Sherpa jackets use a curly high-pile fleece that looks like sheep's wool. They come as full-zip jackets, snap pullovers or as a lining in denim and canvas jackets.
  - q: Do you make quarter-zip fleece pullovers?
    a: Yes. Quarter-zip fleece is popular for corporate and outdoor ranges. We make it with a stand collar, a chest pocket and an embroidered logo.
  - q: Why does some fleece pill and some not?
    a: Pilling depends on the fibre and the finish. Anti-pill fleece has its surface sheared and treated so it stays smooth after washing. We choose anti-pill fleece unless you ask otherwise.
  - q: Can I order fleece in recycled polyester?
    a: Yes. Many fleece mills make fleece from recycled bottles. Any recycled claim on your label rests on the mill's certificate for that fabric.
  - q: Which logo method suits fleece best?
    a: Embroidery is the usual choice because it sits well on the pile. Heat-transfer logos work on smooth microfleece but not on thick or sherpa fleece.
