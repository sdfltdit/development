  - q: What is loopback terry?
    a: Loopback terry has small yarn loops on the inside and a flat face outside. The loops are left unbrushed, so the fabric breathes and stays light.
  - q: Is French terry fabric the same as fleece?
    a: No. Fleece is brushed on the inside until it turns fluffy. French terry fabric keeps its loops, so it is cooler and suits spring and summer.
  - q: Which weight of French terry should I choose?
    a: Light terry suits summer shorts and tees. Midweight suits year-round sweatshirts. Heavyweight gives the structured streetwear look.
  - q: Does French terry shrink?
    a: Cotton terry can shrink. We wash-test the fabric, or garment-dye and wash before final measuring, so the size stays true.
  - q: Can I order private label French terry?
    a: Yes. We add your neck labels, care labels, swing tags and packing, with no trace of our name.
  - q: Can you garment-dye French terry?
    a: Yes. Garment dyeing gives a soft, lived-in colour. We plan for shrinkage in the pattern and dye in our own factory.
