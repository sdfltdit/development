faq:
  - q: Are export cartons included in your price?
    a: Yes. Standard corrugated cartons, tape and shipping marks are included. Printed, hanging or extra-strong cartons are quoted on top.
  - q: What are shipping marks?
    a: The printed details on a carton that identify it, such as buyer, PO number, carton number, weights, size and origin. Your buyer or forwarder usually sets them.
  - q: What is a normal carton weight limit for clothing?
    a: Many US retailers cap cartons at 50 lb, about 23 kg. We pack to the limit in your manual, and weigh every carton.
  - q: How do you choose carton dimensions?
    a: From the folded garment, the pieces per carton and your size limit. We aim for a full, square carton with no empty space.
  - q: Why use 5-ply cartons?
    a: Double-wall 5-ply board survives stacking and damp in a sea container far better than single-wall 3-ply board.
  - q: Can you pack cartons for an Amazon warehouse?
    a: Yes. We follow the fulfilment centre’s packing rules, and each carton gets the shipment label from your seller account.
  - q: Can each carton go to a different shop?
    a: Yes. Send the store list with the quantities for each shop, and we pack and mark one carton per store.
