faq:
  - q: What does a garment merchandiser do?
    a: A garment merchandiser runs your order inside the factory. They quote, plan sampling, pass your comments to each team and follow the order to shipment.
  - q: Will I have a dedicated merchandiser?
    a: Yes. Every order has one dedicated merchandiser, from the first quote to the day the goods ship.
  - q: How does order follow-up work?
    a: You message your merchandiser through our contact form or WhatsApp. The same person answers each time, so nothing gets lost between teams.
  - q: Do you charge for merchandising?
    a: No. Merchandising comes with every order, with no separate fee.
  - q: Can my merchandiser show me the factory?
    a: Yes, by appointment. Book a date through our factory visit form and your merchandiser walks you through the production floor.
  - q: Is apparel merchandising the same as retail merchandising?
    a: No. Apparel merchandising at a factory means running orders from brief to shipment. Retail merchandising means planning and displaying stock in shops.
