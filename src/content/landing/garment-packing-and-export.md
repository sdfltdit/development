faq:
  - q: What packing is included in your price?
    a: Your labels and tags go on, each piece goes in its own polybag, and the order is packed in export cartons. Anything beyond that is quoted to your instructions.
  - q: Can you make custom clothing packaging?
    a: Yes. Branded polybags, tissue, stickers, boxes and hangers are made or sourced to your artwork and quoted with your garments.
  - q: Can you pack to my retailer's instructions?
    a: Yes. Send the packing manual or a sample, and we follow it for polybags, folding, stickers, hangers, assortments and carton marks.
  - q: Which export documents do you provide?
    a: Everything the factory side needs. That covers the commercial invoice, packing list, proof of origin, bill of lading or air waybill, and any other document your LC asks for.
  - q: Do I get a certificate of origin for duty savings?
    a: We provide the certificate of origin or origin statement your importer asks for. Your customs broker confirms whether a lower duty rate applies.
  - q: Do you only ship FOB?
    a: "No. We quote {{incoterm}} {{port}} as standard, and ship on other terms or by air if you ask."
  - q: Who books the freight on an FOB order?
    a: Your freight forwarder books it. We pack, clear the goods for export and hand them over at the port.
