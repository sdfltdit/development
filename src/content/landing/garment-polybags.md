faq:
  - q: Is a polybag included in your price?
    a: Yes. Each piece goes in its own clear polybag at no extra cost. Self-seal, recycled, printed or thicker bags are quoted on top.
  - q: Do my polybags need a suffocation warning?
    a: Often, yes. Five US states, two US cities and most big marketplaces ask for one. We print it on every bag unless you tell us otherwise.
  - q: What thickness should polybags for clothing be?
    a: Most clothing bags are 1.5 to 3 mil, about 38 to 76 microns. Amazon fulfilment asks for at least 1.5 mil.
  - q: Are self-seal polybags worth it?
    a: For online orders, usually yes. They close quickly and neatly, and customers can reseal them for returns.
  - q: Can I use recycled polybags?
    a: Yes. We source bags with recycled film and pass on the bag maker’s documents for any recycled claim you make.
  - q: What is the minimum for branded polybags?

  - q: Do EU packaging rules affect polybags?
    a: Yes. Since 12 August 2026, EU rules cover polybags and limit empty space in packaging. Snug, right-sized bags help you comply.
