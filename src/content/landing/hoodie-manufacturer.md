## Hoodie and sweatshirt styles we make

### Pullover hoodie

Kangaroo pocket, double-layer hood and ribbed cuffs and hem. It is the core style for most brands and the easiest to decorate across the chest and back.

### Full-zip hoodie

A front zip changes the pattern, the trim cost and the decoration area, so we price it as its own style. Tell us the zip type and puller you want, or ask for options.

### Crewneck sweatshirt

The same fleece without the hood. Many brands run a crewneck and a hoodie in one colour programme to share fabric and dyeing.

### Matching joggers

Cut from the same fleece, with an elastic or drawcord waist and cuffed or open hems. Joggers made alongside your hoodies are dyed to the same approved shade.

## Choosing the fleece

The fabric decides how the hoodie feels, how it holds its shape and most of what it costs.

- **Brushed fleece** is warm and soft inside and suits colder markets.
- **French terry** is lighter and more breathable and suits layering and warmer climates.
- **Weight**: our range is {{fleeceGsm}} GSM. Heavier fabric gives a structured, boxy look. Lighter fabric drapes.
- **Composition**: 100% cotton feels premium. Cotton-polyester blends resist pilling and shrinkage and cost less.

We send swatches before sampling, then test the bulk fabric for weight, shrinkage and shade against your approved lab dip before cutting.

## Embroidery, print and trims

Embroidery and screen printing are done in-house, so your logo is digitised, stitched and checked by the same team that sews the garment. Trims such as zips, drawcords, eyelets and labels are sourced to your spec and approved with the sample. Full-colour artwork is printed with DTF, also in-house.

## Fit and sizing

Oversized and boxy hoodies are only as good as the pattern. We grade your size set from your measurement chart, check every measurement on the proto sample and repeat the checks inline during bulk. If you do not have a chart yet, we start from our base block and adjust it with you on the sample.

## What to send for a hoodie quote

Send a reference photo or tech pack, the fabric type and weight, zip or pullover, the decoration and its size, quantity per style and your target {{incoterm}} price. You receive a written quote, sample cost and lead time within {{quoteHours}} hours.
