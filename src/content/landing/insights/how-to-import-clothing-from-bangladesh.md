## How to import clothing from Bangladesh in brief

Bangladesh is one of the largest clothing exporters in the world. Importing from a factory there follows the same path as any overseas order, with a few local details on ports, documents and trade schemes.

This guide walks through how to import clothing from Bangladesh, from first contact to goods in your warehouse. It is general information as of November 2026, not legal or customs advice. Your customs broker confirms duty and paperwork for your market.

## The steps at a glance

| Step | What happens | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Choose a factory | Check it is a real factory and suits your product | You |
| 2. Agree terms | Price, minimum, Incoterm, payment and dates | You and the factory |
| 3. Sample | Proto and pre-production samples approved | Factory, then you |
| 4. Pay | Deposit by bank transfer, or a letter of credit | You and your bank |
| 5. Produce and inspect | Bulk production and final inspection | Factory, plus any inspector you send |
| 6. Ship | Freight booked from Bangladesh to your country | Your forwarder on FOB terms |
| 7. Clear customs | Duty paid and goods released | Your customs broker |

## Step 1: choose the right factory

Start with factories that make your product type every day. Ask for a company profile, check the address and visit or send someone if you can. Our guide on [how to find a clothing manufacturer](/how-to-find-clothing-manufacturer/) lists the questions to ask, and our guide to [clothing manufacturer scams](/insights/avoiding-clothing-manufacturer-scams/) shows the warning signs.

## Step 2: agree price, terms and Incoterm

Most Bangladeshi factories quote FOB. The price covers the goods delivered to the port, cleared for export. You then pay freight, insurance and import costs. Our guide to [Incoterms](/insights/incoterms-explained/) compares FOB with other terms.

Agree these in writing before sampling:

- Price per piece at your quantity.
- Minimum order and how it splits across colours and sizes.
- Sample and bulk lead times.
- Payment terms.
- Packing, labelling and inspection standard.

## Step 3: sample and approve

Every order starts with samples. Approve the fit, fabric, colour and trims before bulk. Samples usually travel by express courier, which is the fastest way to move small parcels out of Bangladesh.

## Step 4: pay safely

Two payment methods are common. A letter of credit protects both sides through the banks. A bank transfer is simpler and cheaper, usually paid in stages. Our guide to [payment terms for clothing orders](/insights/payment-terms-lc-vs-tt/) explains both.

## Step 5: production and inspection

Bulk production follows sample approval. Many buyers book a final inspection before shipment, either their own or a third-party inspector. Payment of the balance often depends on the goods passing it.

## Step 6: shipping from Bangladesh

Most clothing leaves by sea from Chittagong, the country's main port. Air freight leaves from Dhaka and suits urgent or small shipments.

| Mode | Typical use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sea freight, full container | Large orders | Lowest cost per piece |
| Sea freight, shared container | Smaller orders | Your cartons share a container with others |
| Air freight | Urgent orders and top-ups | Much faster, much higher cost per kilo |
| Express courier | Samples and very small orders | Door to door |

Bangladesh sea freight often moves through a transhipment hub before the long ocean leg. Allow several weeks door to door. Our guide to [freight forwarders vs factory shipping](/freight-forwarder-vs-factory-arranged-shipping-vs-diy/) covers who should book.

## The documents you need

The factory prepares the export side. Your broker uses these to clear customs at home.

- **Commercial invoice** with goods, value and terms.
- **Packing list** with cartons, contents and weights.
- **Bill of lading** for sea freight, or an **airway bill** for air.
- **Proof of origin**, as your market and trade scheme require.
- **Any test reports or certificates** your market asks for.

Our guide to [customs clearance](/insights/customs-clearance-guide/) explains what happens when the goods land.

## Import duty from Bangladesh by market

Import duty from Bangladesh depends on where you sell and whether the goods meet the rules of origin. Always confirm the rate and the HS code with your broker. Our [HS code finder](/tools/hs-code/) helps you find the right heading.

| Market | Trade status for Bangladesh clothing |
|---|---|
| European Union | Duty-free under the EBA scheme when origin rules are met |
| United Kingdom | Preferential access under the Developing Countries Trading Scheme |
| Canada, Australia and Japan | Least developed country access, with transition periods agreed |
| United States | No general preference for clothing, standard duty applies |

The EBA scheme is the EU's trade scheme for least developed countries. Bangladesh's least developed country graduation was moved from November 2026 to 24 November 2029. Most markets then give a transition period before preferences change, so check the position each year.

## Landed cost

The factory price is only part of the cost. Freight, insurance, duty, broker fees and inland delivery all add up. Our guide on [how to calculate landed cost](/insights/how-to-calculate-landed-cost/) shows the full sum.
