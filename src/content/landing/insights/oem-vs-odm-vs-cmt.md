## The terms buyers hear first

When brands start talking to factories, they hear OEM, ODM, CMT, CM and FOB. These terms describe who supplies the design, who buys the fabric and what the price includes. Mixing them up can lead to quotes that cannot be compared.

This guide explains each of these terms in plain language.

## OEM vs ODM vs CMT at a glance

| Model | Design from | Fabric from | Trims from | Price covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OEM, FOB | You | Factory | Factory | Everything to the port |
| ODM | Factory | Factory | Factory | Everything to the port |
| CMT | You | You | Factory | Cutting, sewing, trims, packing |
| CM only | You | You | You | Cutting and sewing |

## OEM, your design made by the factory

OEM stands for original equipment manufacturer. In clothing, it means the factory makes your design to your spec. You send a tech pack or sample, and the factory sources materials, samples, produces and packs.

OEM is the most common model for brands. It is very close to what most people call private label.

## ODM, the factory's design with your brand

ODM stands for original design manufacturer. An ODM clothing manufacturer has its own catalogue of designs. You choose a style, pick colours and add your labels.

ODM is fast and needs little design work. The trade-offs are that other brands may sell the same style, and design ownership usually stays with the factory.

## CMT and CM only

CMT means cut, make, trim. You buy the fabric and send it to the factory. The factory cuts, sews, adds trims and packs.

CM only production goes one step further. You supply both fabric and trims, and the factory only cuts and sews.

These models suit brands that already have strong fabric suppliers. They also add work, because you must manage fabric quality, timing and shipping to the factory.

## FOB vs CMT pricing

On FOB vs CMT, the key point is what the price includes:

| Cost | FOB price | CMT price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fabric | Included | Paid by you |
| Trims | Included | Included |
| Labour | Included | Included |
| Fabric waste and shortage risk | Factory | You |
| Fabric freight to factory | Factory | You |

A CMT price always looks lower. To compare fairly, add your fabric cost, fabric freight and waste allowance. Our guide on [Incoterms](/insights/incoterms-explained/) explains what FOB covers.

## Design ownership

Design ownership matters if you plan to grow a brand:

- **OEM, CMT and CM only.** The design is yours. Ask for a non-disclosure agreement if you want extra protection.
- **ODM.** The design usually belongs to the factory. If you need it exclusive, agree that in writing before you order.

## How to choose

| Your situation | Usual best fit |
| --- | --- |
| New brand with a design idea | OEM on FOB terms |
| Need stock fast, no design team | ODM |
| You already buy your own fabric | CMT |
| You control fabric and trims | CM only |
