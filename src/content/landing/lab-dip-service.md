faq:
  - q: What is a lab dip service?
    a: A lab dip service dyes a small swatch of your fabric to match a colour reference. You approve the closest option before any bulk fabric is dyed.
  - q: How does lab dip approval work?
    a: We send two or three options per colour, marked A, B and C. You pick one, ask for changes or reject them all, and we redo them.
  - q: Can you do Pantone colour matching on any fabric?
    a: Yes, but each fabric takes dye differently. Polyester and cotton dyed to one Pantone can look slightly different, so we match each fabric to the same colour standard.
  - q: What is a colour standard?
    a: A colour standard is the approved swatch that every bulk roll is compared with. It stays on file for reorders.
  - q: How close is shade matching?
    a: Bulk is checked against the colour standard under the same light. Small differences are normal, and we agree the limit before bulk.
  - q: Do I need lab dips for every colour?
    a: Yes, for every colour dyed for your order. Stock colours from the mill can use the mill's swatch instead.
  - q: Can I send a fabric swatch instead of a Pantone code?
    a: Yes. A physical swatch often matches better than a code, because screens and print show colour differently.
