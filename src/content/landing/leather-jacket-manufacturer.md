  - q: Which leather is best for a jacket?
    a: Cowhide is thick and hard wearing, so it suits biker jackets. Lambskin is soft and light, for fashion jackets. Goatskin sits between the two. Suede gives a brushed, matte look.
  - q: Do you make faux leather jackets?
    a: Yes. Faux leather jackets are made from PU or other coated fabrics. They cost less, are easier to keep consistent in colour and suit vegan ranges.
  - q: Do you make biker jackets?
    a: Yes. The classic biker shape has an off-centre zip and wide lapels that snap down, and we cut it in cowhide, lambskin or faux leather.
  - q: Why do leather hides vary in colour?
    a: Each hide is natural, so grain and shade vary slightly. We sort hides by shade before cutting so panels on one jacket match.
