faq:
  - q: What is the minimum order for linen clothing?
    a: We need {{moq}} {{moqUnit}} for each linen style in one fabric. Those pieces can be split across colours and sizes, as set out on your quote.
  - q: How much does linen shrink?
    a: Untreated linen often shrinks around 4 per cent on the first wash and can shrink more. We wash-test the bulk fabric and add the right allowance to the pattern before cutting.
  - q: What is pre-washed linen?
    a: Pre-washed linen is washed at the fabric mill before it reaches us. It feels softer, creases in a relaxed way and shrinks far less once made up.
  - q: Should I choose pure linen or a linen blend?
    a: Pure linen is the most breathable and has the classic texture. A linen blend with cotton creases less and costs less. A linen-viscose blend drapes more softly for dresses.
  - q: Can I order private label linen clothing?
    a: Yes. Private label linen clothing leaves our factory with your labels, hang tags and packing. Our name does not appear on it.
  - q: Why does linen fabric have thick and thin lines?
    a: Those slubs come from the natural flax yarn. They are normal in linen and not a fault. We agree how much slub is acceptable on the swatch before bulk.
  - q: Do you make custom linen clothing for menswear?
    a: Yes. Linen shirts, camp-collar shirts, drawstring trousers and shorts are common menswear orders, along with dresses and co-ords for womenswear.
