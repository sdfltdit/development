  - q: What is the minimum order for custom bras?
    a: Bras start at {{moq}} {{moqUnit}} per style in one fabric. Band and cup sizes inside that style share the total, which matters because bras have many more sizes than tops.
  - q: Are you a bra manufacturer for fuller-bust sizes?
    a: Yes. We grade bras across band and cup sizes from your chart, including fuller-bust sizes. Larger cups often need wider wings, stronger straps and a firmer centre gore, which we set on the fit sample.
  - q: Do you make underwire bras?
    a: Yes. Underwire bras have a wire in a padded channel under each cup. We match the wire shape and size to each cup size and check that it sits flat against the body.
  - q: What is the difference between moulded and cut-and-sew cups?
    a: Moulded cups are shaped foam with no seams, so they look smooth under T-shirts. Cut-and-sew cups are built from seamed panels, which allows lace and gives more lift for fuller sizes.
  - q: Can private label lingerie carry our own labels?
    a: Yes. Private label lingerie can have printed or satin labels, branded hooks and charms, and retail boxes or bags. Our name does not appear on it.
  - q: Do you make matching lingerie sets?
    a: Yes. We cut bras and briefs from the same lace and fabric lot, so the shade matches. Sets are usually sold as separate styles so customers can mix sizes.
  - q: How many sizes should a first bra range have?
    a: Many brands start with eight to twelve core band and cup sizes, then add more once they see what sells. Every size needs stock, so a tight first range keeps money free.
