## How low MOQ clothing manufacturing works with us

Our minimum is {{moq}} {{moqUnit}}. One style means one pattern in one fabric. If two products share a pattern but use different fabrics, they are two styles. Decoration, such as a print or embroidery, does not create a new style, but it is priced separately.

The part most factories leave vague is how the minimum splits across colours and sizes. That depends on the fabric mill's dye-lot minimum, not on our sewing lines, so we confirm it per fabric on your written quote. You know the exact split before you pay for a sample.

## Why {{moq}} pieces, and not a few dozen

Small workshops can sew a few dozen pieces, but they rarely dye fabric to your colour, grade a full size set, or inspect to a recognised standard. Large export factories do all of that but usually start in the thousands. Our minimum sits in between: low enough for a launch or a capsule, high enough to run on a proper production line with lab dips, graded patterns and AQL {{aql}} inspection.

## What low MOQ costs you, honestly

Fixed costs are shared across fewer pieces, so the price per piece is higher at {{moq}} than at 1,000. The price table on this page shows that gap for a standard cotton T-shirt. For many brands the higher unit price is still the better deal, because it means less money tied up in stock and less risk if a colour does not sell.

## Who low MOQ production suits

- **New brands** testing a first collection before committing to volume
- **Established brands** launching a capsule, a collaboration or a new category
- **Online stores** that want factory quality without a container of stock
- **Merch and uniform programmes** with a fixed, known quantity

If you need fewer than {{moq}} pieces per style, we will tell you so up front rather than quote a price that does not work for you.

## How to make a small order go further

1. **Keep colours to the fabric's dye-lot minimum**, so you avoid small-lot surcharges.
2. **Share fabric across styles**, for example a hoodie and joggers in the same fleece.
3. **Lock the fit on the sample** so reorders run without a new sample round.
4. **Plan reorders early**. An approved style goes into production faster the second time.
