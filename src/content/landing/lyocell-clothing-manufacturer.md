  - q: Is lyocell fabric the same as Tencel?
    a: Tencel is a brand name for lyocell owned by a fibre company. You can only use that name when the mill supplies that branded fibre under licence and the brand owner approves your labels. Otherwise the label says lyocell.
  - q: What is a lyocell blend good for?
    a: A lyocell blend with cotton costs less and feels crisper. Lyocell with linen creases less than pure linen. Lyocell with spandex adds stretch for fitted pieces.
  - q: Does lyocell shrink?
    a: Lyocell can shrink a little and may change feel after the first wash. We wash-test the fabric and allow for it in the pattern.
  - q: Why does lyocell get white marks?
    a: Lyocell can show chalky marks where it rubs while wet, called fibrillation. Mills treat the fabric against it, and we handle it gently through washing.
  - q: Can I order private label lyocell clothing?
    a: Yes. Every piece carries your labels, swing tags and packing, never our name.
  - q: Can I call lyocell sustainable?
    a: Only with the mill's paperwork. We pass on the mill's documents and make no green claims ourselves.
