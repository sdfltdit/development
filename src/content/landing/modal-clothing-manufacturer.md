  - q: What is micro modal?
    a: Micro modal is modal spun from much finer fibres. It feels lighter and silkier, so it suits underwear and close-fitting tops.
  - q: Is a modal blend better than pure modal?
    a: It depends on use. A modal blend with cotton costs less and holds shape well. Modal with spandex adds stretch for underwear and leggings.
  - q: What is modal made from?
    a: Modal is a type of viscose, usually made from beech wood pulp. The mill's report confirms the fibre source and content.
  - q: Does modal shrink or pill?
    a: Modal shrinks less than cotton and resists pilling well. We still wash-test every fabric before cutting.
  - q: Can I order private label modal clothing?

  - q: How should modal clothing be washed?
