  - q: What is nylon spandex?
    a: A knitted fabric of nylon with elastane. It feels smooth and cool and recovers its shape, so it suits leggings, sports bras and swimwear.
  - q: Is polyamide fabric the same as nylon?
    a: Yes. Polyamide is the fibre name used on UK and EU labels, and nylon is the common name used in the US.
  - q: Nylon or polyester for activewear?
    a: Nylon feels softer and resists abrasion better. Polyester costs less, dries fast and can be sublimation printed. Nylon cannot take sublimation print.
  - q: Do you make nylon jackets?
    a: Yes. Windbreakers, coach jackets and light shells in nylon taslan or ripstop, with linings, zips and taped seams where the design needs them.
  - q: Can you make custom nylon clothing in my own colour?
    a: Yes. Your colour is matched by lab dip on the actual nylon before bulk, because nylon takes dye differently from polyester.
