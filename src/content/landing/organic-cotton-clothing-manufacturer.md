faq:
  - q: How do I know the fabric is really organic cotton?
    a: The fabric mill holds the certificate and issues documents for each delivery. We pass these to you with the order, so the claim rests on the mill's records.
  - q: What is an organic cotton certificate?
    a: It is a document from a certification body confirming that the fabric was made from organic fibre under its rules. Shipments are often backed by a transaction certificate as well.
  - q: Can my finished garments carry an organic label?
    a: That depends on the scheme and your market. Some labels need every stage of production certified. Tell us at quote stage, and we confirm what is possible for your order.
  - q: Are organic cotton t-shirts more expensive?
    a: Usually a little, because organic fabric costs more than conventional cotton. Your quote shows the difference for your fabric and weight.
  - q: Can I order custom organic clothing in my own colours?
    a: Yes, within the mill's dye lot for each colour. Lab dips are matched to your Pantone or swatch before bulk fabric is dyed.
  - q: Do you print on organic cotton?
    a: Yes. Water-based and discharge inks keep a soft hand on organic cotton, and embroidery is done in-house too.
