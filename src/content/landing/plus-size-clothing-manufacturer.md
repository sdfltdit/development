  - q: How is plus size grading different from normal grading?
    a: Plus size grading uses its own base size in the plus range. Bodies change shape as sizes rise, so stretching a small base size upward leaves armholes tight and hems short. We fit the plus base size first, then grade from it.
  - q: Can you add extended sizes to my existing range?
    a: Yes. Send your current size chart and a sample. We draft a plus base size, make a fit sample for you to try on a plus-size fitter, and grade the rest from there.
  - q: Why do plus sizes cost more per piece?
    a: Larger sizes use more fabric, and a separate grade takes more pattern work. We show the price for straight and extended sizes on your quote so you can plan retail prices.
  - q: Can I order private label plus size clothing with my own labels?
    a: Yes. Every piece leaves our factory with your labels, hang tags and packing. Our name does not appear on it.
  - q: What does size inclusive clothing need from a factory?
    a: It needs a factory that grades plus sizes separately and scales prints and pockets so they look right on every size. It also needs a real plus-size fitter, so we ask you to try the fit sample on one before bulk.
  - q: Do you make plus size activewear?
    a: Yes. Leggings, sports bras and training tops can be graded into extended sizes. Fabric with good recovery and opacity matters even more in larger sizes.
