  - q: Can you make recycled polyester clothing?

  - q: Is poly-cotton clothing better than pure polyester?
    a: It depends on use. Poly-cotton clothing feels softer and more like cotton. Pure polyester dries faster and takes sublimation best.
  - q: Why does polyester colour look different from cotton?
    a: Polyester takes disperse dyes and cotton takes reactive dyes, so one shade can look different on each. We match both to one lab dip and check them side by side.
  - q: Can you sublimate on polyester?
    a: Yes. Sublimation works on white or light polyester and prints edge to edge without cracking.
  - q: Does polyester pill?
    a: Some fabrics pill more than others. We choose yarns and finishes that resist pilling and test the fabric before bulk.
