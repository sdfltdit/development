faq:
  - q: What is the minimum order for a clothing printing service?
    a: Printing is part of a garment order, which starts at {{moq}} {{moqUnit}} per style. One design can run across every colour and size in that order.
  - q: Is screen printing or DTF printing better?
    a: Screen printing is best for solid logos in a few colours and large runs. DTF printing suits full-colour artwork and small details. We advise on the sample.
  - q: What is puff print?
    a: Puff print uses an ink that rises with heat, so the design feels raised. It suits bold logos on hoodies and sweats.
  - q: Why choose water-based ink?
    a: Water-based ink soaks into the fabric, so the print feels soft and breathes. It works best on light cotton or with discharge on dark cotton.
  - q: Will prints crack after washing?
    a: Not when the ink suits the fabric and is cured right. We wash-test printed samples and stretch-test prints before bulk.
  - q: Can you print all-over designs on cotton?
    a: Yes, as fabric printed at the mill before cutting. For polyester, we sublimate in our own factory.
  - q: How many colours can a screen print have?
    a: Most designs use one to eight colours. Each colour needs its own screen, which shows on the quote.
