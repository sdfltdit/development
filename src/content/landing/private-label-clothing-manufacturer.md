## What private label clothing manufacturing includes

Private label means the product is yours from the inside out. We work from your design or reference, develop the pattern to your measurements, source the fabric and trims you approve, and finish every piece with your branding.

- **Pattern and fit** developed from your measurement chart or reference garment
- **Fabric and colour** sourced to your spec and matched to your lab dip
- **Branding** on neck labels, care labels, swing tags and the garment itself
- **Packaging** in individual poly bags with your barcode or sticker
- **Inspection** inline and at final AQL {{aql}} before shipment

## Branding options

### Neck labels

Woven labels give a premium finish. Printed (tagless) labels feel softer and suit T-shirts. Both are produced to your artwork and approved with the sample.

### Care and content labels

Required by law in most markets. We print the fibre content, care symbols and country of origin to the rules of your destination market and confirm the wording with you.

### Logo decoration

Screen printing and embroidery are done in-house, so your logo is placed, sized and colour-checked by the same team that makes the garment.

### Tags and packaging

Swing tags, barcodes, size stickers and poly bags are added at the packing stage, so cartons arrive ready for your warehouse or fulfilment centre.

## How we protect your designs

Your files are used only for your orders and shared only with the people making them. An NDA can be signed before you send a tech pack. Patterns and artwork stay on file for your reorders and are never offered to other buyers.

## When private label is the right model

Private label suits brands that want a product customers cannot buy elsewhere: their own fit, fabric, colour and label. If you need a few dozen blank garments with a logo, a print-on-demand or blank-wholesale supplier is usually faster and cheaper, and we will tell you so.
