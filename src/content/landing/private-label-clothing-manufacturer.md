faq:
  - q: What is private label clothing?
    a: It is clothing made by a factory and sold under your brand. You own the design, fit and branding, and the maker stays invisible to your customers.
  - q: Do you offer white label clothing from stock?
    a: No. We do not hold stock garments. Every piece is cut and sewn for your order, to your design and fit.
  - q: Is an OEM clothing manufacturer the same as private label?
    a: In clothing, yes. OEM means the factory makes to the buyer's specification, which is how private label works.
  - q: Will my designs be shared with other brands?
    a: No. Tech packs, patterns and artwork are used only for your orders. Ask us to sign an NDA before you send files.
  - q: Who decides the wording on my care labels?
    a: You do. We print the fibre content, care wording and origin you approve on the sample. Labelling rules differ by market, so check them for each country you sell in.
