  - q: Should I choose down or synthetic padded jackets?
    a: Down is lighter and warmer for its weight but costs more and loses warmth when wet. Synthetic padded jackets cost less, stay warm when damp and are easier to wash.
  - q: Do you make down jackets?
    a: Yes. We fill down jackets with duck or goose down from the supplier you choose. That supplier sets the fill power and provides any paperwork on where the down comes from.
  - q: How do you stop down leaking through the shell?
    a: We use a down-proof shell or a down-proof lining, fine needles and tight stitching along the quilt lines. We check each jacket for leaking fill before packing.
  - q: Do you make puffer vests?
    a: Yes. Puffer vests use the same fill and quilting as our jackets, with bound armholes and a stand collar. They suit layering ranges and corporate gifts.
  - q: Are your puffer jackets waterproof?
    a: They can be. A water-repellent shell keeps light rain out. For fully waterproof puffers, we use a waterproof shell and seal the seams with tape, which adds cost.
  - q: How are puffer jackets packed for shipping?
    a: Puffers are folded and packed in bags, then lightly compressed in cartons to save freight. They recover their loft once unpacked.
