faq:
  - q: Is garment quality inspection included in the price?
    a: Yes. Fabric checks, inline inspection and final inspection at AQL {{aql}} are part of every order we produce.
  - q: What is inline inspection?
    a: Inline inspection checks garments while they are being sewn. It catches faults early, before a whole order has the same problem.
  - q: What is a final random inspection?
    a: A final random inspection pulls a set number of packed pieces at random and checks them. The AQL level sets how many faults are allowed.
  - q: Can I book my own pre-shipment inspection?
    a: Yes. You can send your own inspector or an inspection company to check goods before shipment. We give them space and the packed cartons.
  - q: What does garment quality control cover?
    a: Garment quality control covers fabric, cutting, sewing, measurements, prints, trims, labels and packing. Each stage has its own checks.
  - q: What happens if an inspection fails?
    a: We sort and repair the goods, then inspect again before shipping. You get the report for both inspections.
  - q: Do I get photos?
    a: Yes. The final report includes photos of the goods, measurements and packing.
