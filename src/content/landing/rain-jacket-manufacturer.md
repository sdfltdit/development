  - q: What is the minimum order for custom rain jackets?

  - q: What makes a jacket waterproof rather than water resistant?
    a: A waterproof jacket needs a waterproof fabric and sealed seams. Water-resistant fabric sheds light rain but leaks under pressure, and stitched seams leak through the needle holes unless they are taped.
  - q: Are you a waterproof jacket manufacturer with seam taping in-house?
    a: Yes. We tape seams on our own hot-air taping machines. Fully taped jackets have every seam sealed, while critical-seam taping covers only the shoulders and hood to save cost.
  - q: What is hydrostatic head?
    a: It is a lab test that shows how much water pressure a fabric can take before it leaks, measured in millimetres. The number comes from the fabric mill's test report, and we use it for any rating on your label.
  - q: Do you make seam-taped jackets in breathable fabric?
    a: Yes. Laminated 2.5-layer and 3-layer shells let sweat vapour out while keeping rain out. They cost more than PU-coated fabric but feel less clammy.
  - q: Can a rain jacket carry an embroidered logo?
    a: Yes, but embroidery makes holes in the shell. We tape the back of the embroidery or use heat-transfer logos so the jacket stays waterproof.
  - q: Do you make rainwear for kids?
    a: Yes. Kids' rain jackets and dungarees use the same taped construction, with reflective details and easy fastenings. Your lab handles any testing your market needs for children's wear.
