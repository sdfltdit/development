faq:
  - q: What is recycled fabric clothing made from?
    a: Most recycled polyester comes from used plastic bottles. Recycled nylon often comes from industrial waste or old fishing nets. Recycled cotton comes from cutting scraps or old garments.
  - q: How do I prove recycled content claims?
    a: Use the share stated on the mill's certificate, such as a fabric with a set percentage of recycled polyester. We pass the documents to you with the order.
  - q: Is recycled nylon as strong as new nylon?
    a: Good recycled nylon performs much like new nylon for most garments. We check stretch and recovery on the sample before you approve bulk.
  - q: Why is recycled cotton usually blended?
    a: Recycling shortens cotton fibres, so recycled cotton is blended with new or other fibres to keep the fabric strong.
  - q: Can trims and labels be recycled too?
    a: Often yes. Recycled zip tape, thread and printed labels are available from some suppliers. We list the options on your quote.
  - q: Does recycled polyester cost more?
    a: Usually a little more than new polyester. Your quote shows the price for your fabric, weight and colour.
