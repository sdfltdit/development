  - q: What is ripstop fabric?
    a: A woven fabric with thicker yarns woven in at regular intervals to form a grid. If the fabric is cut or torn, the grid stops the tear from spreading.
  - q: Nylon ripstop or cotton ripstop?
    a: Nylon ripstop is light and strong and suits jackets and windbreakers. Cotton ripstop is softer and breathes better, so it suits cargo pants, shorts and overshirts.
  - q: Do you make ripstop cargo pants?
    a: Yes. Cargo pants with bellows pockets, adjustable hems and bar-tacked stress points are among our most common ripstop orders.
  - q: Is ripstop waterproof?
    a: Not on its own. Many ripstops carry a water-repellent finish, and its rating comes from the mill's report. A waterproof garment also needs taped seams.
  - q: Can I have ripstop in my own colour?
    a: Yes. Your colour is matched by lab dip before bulk, and cotton ripstop can also be garment dyed after sewing.
