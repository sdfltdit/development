## The two samples that matter

### Proto sample

The first physical version of your design. It answers one question: is the shape, fit and construction right? It may be made in a similar fabric if your exact fabric is not yet dyed. You review it, send comments and we revise if needed.

### Pre-production sample

Made from the actual bulk fabric with your real labels and trims. It is the reference the bulk order is measured against, so we do not start cutting until you have signed it off in writing.

## How to review a sample

1. **Measure it** against your spec at the points of measure, and compare with our measurement report.
2. **Wash it** once or twice as your customers would, then measure again.
3. **Wear it** for a day to check comfort, neckline and sleeve length.
4. **Check the details**: stitching, label placement, print position and colour.
5. **Send written comments** with photos, so the revision is exact.

## What drives the sample fee

The fee covers pattern making, the fabric and trims used and the operator time on a one-off garment, which is much slower than bulk. Decoration such as embroidery or a new print screen adds to it. Under the sample fee policy above, a sample that leads to production works like a deposit on that order.
