  - q: Can you match our school colours exactly?
    a: Yes, within normal dye tolerance. Send a colour reference such as a Pantone code or an old garment. We send a lab dip for approval and keep the approved shade on file for future orders.
  - q: Do you make school polo shirts with an embroidered crest?
    a: Yes. School polo shirts are our most ordered uniform item. The crest is embroidered on our own machines, and we match thread colours to your crest artwork.
  - q: Do you make PE kits?
    a: Yes. PE kits usually mean a sports T-shirt or polo, shorts or skorts and a tracksuit. We make them in polyester sports knits or cotton blends, with printed or embroidered logos.
  - q: Are school sweatshirts made in fleece?
    a: Yes. School sweatshirts are usually poly-cotton fleece, which keeps its colour and resists pilling better than pure cotton. Crew neck and V-neck styles both work.
  - q: When should we order for the new school year?
