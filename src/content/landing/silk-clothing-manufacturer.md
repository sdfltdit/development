faq:
  - q: Do you work with real silk or only satin?
    a: Both. We make garments in mulberry silk and in polyester or blended satin. Satin is a weave, so the fibre is always stated on the quote and the label.
  - q: What is silk momme weight?
    a: Momme is the traditional weight unit for silk. Higher numbers mean a heavier, more opaque silk. We take the momme and grade from the mill's test report for your fabric.
  - q: Are you also a satin clothing manufacturer?
    a: Yes. Polyester satin costs less, washes more easily and takes sublimation prints. It suits sleepwear, linings and fashion pieces at a lower price.
  - q: How do you stop silk from puckering at the seams?
    a: We use fine needles, sharp thread, adjusted tension and French seams. Every style is checked on the proto sample before bulk.
  - q: Can I order mulberry silk clothing in my own colours?
    a: Yes, subject to the mill's dye lot for each colour. Lab dips are matched to your Pantone or swatch before the fabric is dyed.
  - q: How should silk clothing be labelled for care?
    a: Most silk is labelled for hand wash or dry clean. The care wording is confirmed after a wash test on the sample, and you approve the label.
