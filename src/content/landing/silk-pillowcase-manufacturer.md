  - q: Which momme is best for mulberry silk pillowcases?
    a: Most brands choose 19 or 22 momme. Higher momme means more silk per square metre, so the fabric is heavier and lasts longer, and it also costs more. 25 momme suits a top-tier line.
  - q: What is the difference between silk and satin pillowcases?
    a: Silk is a natural fibre. Satin is a weave, usually made from polyester. Satin pillowcases feel smooth and cost far less, but they must be labelled as polyester, not silk.
  - q: Envelope closure or hidden zip?
    a: An envelope closure has no hardware, so nothing can snag hair. A hidden zip keeps the pillow firmly inside. Many brands offer the envelope style as standard.
