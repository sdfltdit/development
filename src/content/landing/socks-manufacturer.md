  - q: What is the minimum order for custom socks?
    a: Our minimum is {{moq}} pairs per design. Colourways of the same design and the size split are agreed on your written quote.
  - q: What does needle count mean on socks?
    a: It tells you how fine the knit is. Fewer needles give a thicker, chunkier sock, which suits athletic and hiking socks. More needles give a finer, smoother knit for dress socks.
  - q: Can private label socks have our logo knitted in?
    a: Yes. Private label socks can carry a knit-in jacquard logo on the cuff, leg or sole. Knit-in logos last as long as the sock, unlike printed logos.
  - q: Do you make athletic socks with cushioning?
    a: Yes. Athletic socks can have terry cushioning under the foot, arch support bands, mesh panels for airflow and a reinforced heel and toe.
  - q: Which yarn is best for crew socks?
    a: Combed cotton with a little nylon and spandex is the usual choice for everyday crew socks. Nylon adds strength at the heel and toe, and spandex keeps the cuff up.
  - q: Do you make socks in packs?
    a: Yes. Socks can be packed in pairs, three-packs or larger multipacks, with paper bands, header cards or boxes printed with your branding.
  - q: How are sock sizes set?
    a: Socks are usually sized in ranges, such as small, medium and large, each covering several shoe sizes. We set the foot length and leg length for each range from your size chart.
