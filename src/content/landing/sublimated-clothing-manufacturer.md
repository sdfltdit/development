  - q: How does sublimation printing work?
    a: Sublimation printing turns dye into a gas with heat and pressure. The dye bonds into the polyester, so the print cannot crack or peel.
  - q: Can all-over print clothing be made in cotton?
    a: Not by sublimation. Sublimation needs polyester. For cotton, we use screen print, DTF or fabric printed at the mill.
  - q: Is dye sublimation apparel colourfast?
    a: Yes. The dye sits inside the fibre, so colours stay bright wash after wash. We test a sample for fading before bulk.
  - q: Why do sublimated pieces sometimes show white lines?
    a: White lines appear when fabric folds during pressing. We print on flat cut panels before sewing, which prevents them.
  - q: Can each piece have a different name and number?
    a: Yes. Names and numbers are printed into each piece at no extra setup, so team orders are easy.
  - q: Can you sublimate dark fabric?
    a: No. Sublimation dye cannot cover dark fabric. We print the dark colour as part of the design on white polyester.
