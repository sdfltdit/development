    a: All cutting, sewing, printing and embroidery happen in our own factory, with no subcontractors. Buyer audits are welcome during order development, so you can check for yourself.

  - q: Can I call my clothing eco-friendly?
    a: Be careful. In the EU, broad words like that are now only allowed with recognised, outstanding environmental performance behind them. A specific claim, such as the share of organic cotton, is safer.
