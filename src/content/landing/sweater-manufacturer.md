  - q: What does gauge mean on a sweater?
    a: It describes how fine the knit is. A low gauge gives a chunky, warm knit. A high gauge gives a fine, smooth knit for layering and office wear.
  - q: Are you a knitwear manufacturer or a cut-and-sew factory?
    a: Both. We knit sweaters on flat knitting machines and link the panels, and we also sew cut-and-sew knits such as hoodies and tees. Each method has its own page.
  - q: Are you a cardigan manufacturer too?
    a: Yes. We make button, zip and open-front cardigans, with rib or knitted button bands, pockets and horn or wooden buttons.
  - q: What is fully fashioned knitting?
    a: The machine shapes each panel to size by adding and dropping stitches, so there is little waste. The panels are then linked stitch by stitch for flat, stretchy seams.
  - q: Can private label knitwear carry a knitted-in logo?
    a: Yes. Private label knitwear can have a jacquard or intarsia logo knitted into the body, or a woven label, embroidery or patch added after knitting.
  - q: How do you stop sweaters pilling?
    a: Pilling depends mostly on the yarn. Longer fibres and tighter twist pill less. We advise on yarn choice and test pilling on the swatch before bulk.
