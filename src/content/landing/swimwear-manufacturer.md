  - q: Which fabric is best for swimwear?
    a: Nylon-spandex feels softest and suits fashion swimwear. Polyester-spandex holds colour better and takes sublimation prints. For pool training, choose a chlorine-resistant polyester from the mill.
  - q: Is your swimwear chlorine resistant or UV protective?
    a: Those properties come from the fabric, not from sewing. Choose a fabric the mill has tested for chlorine or UV, and its test report goes with your order.
  - q: Do you make swim shorts for men?
