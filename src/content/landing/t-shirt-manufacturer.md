## T-shirt styles we make

Most brands come to us with one of four briefs. Each is cut from your measurement chart, so the fit you approve on the sample is the fit you get in bulk.

### Classic crew neck

The everyday tee: regular body, set-in sleeves, 1×1 rib collar. It is the most price-efficient style and the one most brands launch with.

### Boxy and oversized

Wider through the chest, often shorter in the body, with dropped shoulders. "Boxy", "oversized" and "relaxed" mean different things to different brands, so we work from your point-of-measure chart rather than a label. Send the chest width, body length and shoulder drop you want and we pattern to it.

### Long sleeve and tank tops

Same jersey and construction, with cuffs or armhole binding. They can run in the same colours as your short-sleeve style to share fabric dyeing.

### Kids' T-shirts

Smaller size sets, softer jersey and child-safe trims. Tell us the destination market so labels and any drawstring rules are handled correctly.

## Fabrics and weights

Fabric drives most of the price and most of the feel. Our standard range is {{tshirtGsm}} GSM. As a rough guide, 140–160 GSM feels light and drapes well, 180 GSM is the classic retail weight, and 200–220 GSM feels substantial and holds a boxy shape. GSM measures grams per square metre. If you work in ounces, 180 GSM is about 5.3 oz per square yard.

- **100% combed cotton** for a smooth face and clean prints
- **CVC (cotton-rich blend)** to reduce shrinkage and cost
- **Polyester blends** for sports and promotional tees

Before cutting, we test the bulk fabric for shrinkage and colour against your approved lab dip, so the second wash does not change the size you sold.

## Printing and embroidery

Screen printing and embroidery happen under our roof, which keeps colour control and timelines in our hands. Send your artwork with the print size and Pantone references, and we confirm the ink type and any special effect on your quote. For photographic or full-colour artwork, we use DTF or sublimation printing, also in-house.

## How we keep bulk the same as your sample

The most common complaint about overseas production is a bulk order that does not match the approved sample. Our process is built around that risk:

1. **Lab dip approval** before fabric is dyed, matched to your Pantone or physical swatch
2. **Bulk fabric testing** for weight, shrinkage and colour before cutting
3. **Pre-production sample** made from the bulk fabric, signed off by you
4. **Inline checks** on the sewing lines for measurements and stitching
5. **Final AQL {{aql}} inspection**, with the report shared before shipment

## What to send for an accurate quote

A tech pack gives the most accurate price, but you can start with less. Send a reference photo, your target fabric and weight, the decoration, the quantity per style and your target {{incoterm}} price. We reply with a written quote, sample cost and lead time within {{quoteHours}} hours.
