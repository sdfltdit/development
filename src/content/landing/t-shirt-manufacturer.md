  - q: What GSM should I choose for my T-shirts?
    a: Around 160–180 GSM suits everyday tees and merch. Heavyweight T-shirts at 200–240 GSM feel thicker and hold their shape. Oversized T-shirts for streetwear usually use 240 GSM or more. If you are unsure, we can send swatches in two or three weights.
  - q: Which printing method is best for T-shirts?
    a: Screen printing suits bold designs with a few colours and gives the lowest price in bulk. DTF suits full-colour artwork and works on cotton and polyester. Sublimation works on light polyester fabric. Embroidery suits small logos, especially on polo shirts.
  - q: Are you also a polo shirt manufacturer?

  - q: How do you stop T-shirts shrinking or twisting after washing?
    a: We wash-test the bulk fabric before cutting and agree the shrinkage limit with you in writing. Side seams are checked for twist after the wash test, and the pre-production sample is washed too.
  - q: Do you make private label T-shirts?
    a: Yes. Your brand goes on the neck label, care label, hang tag and poly bag, in woven or printed form. Our name never appears on your T-shirts.
  - q: Do you sell blank T-shirts?
    a: No. We are a custom T-shirt manufacturer, so we make every order to the buyer's spec and hold no blank stock. Every order starts from your fabric, fit and colour.
  - q: What should I ask a T-shirt manufacturer before ordering?
