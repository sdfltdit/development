faq:
  - q: Do you make tech packs without a production order?
    a: No. We make tech packs for styles we will sample and produce, from {{moq}} {{moqUnit}}. That keeps the pack tied to a real sample.
  - q: What is in a clothing tech pack?
    a: A clothing tech pack holds flat sketches, measurements, a size chart, fabrics, trims, colours, artwork placement and labels. It tells the factory exactly what to make.
  - q: Is a spec sheet the same as a tech pack?
    a: A spec sheet is one part of a tech pack. It lists the measurements for each size, while the tech pack also covers materials, colours and construction.
  - q: What is a bill of materials?
    a: A bill of materials lists every fabric, trim and label in the garment, with colours and amounts. It sets cost and avoids missing parts.
  - q: What do I need to start tech pack design?
    a: A sketch, photos of garments you like or a sample to copy, plus any colours and logos. We fill in the technical detail.
  - q: Can I bring my own tech pack?
    a: Yes. We check it, point out anything missing, and quote from it within {{quoteHours}} hours.
  - q: Will you share my tech pack?
    a: No. We never share your files, and we can sign an NDA before you send them.
