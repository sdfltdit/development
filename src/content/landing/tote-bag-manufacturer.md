  - q: What weight should canvas tote bags be?

  - q: Cotton tote bags or canvas?
    a: Cotton tote bags use thinner fabric and cost less. Canvas is a thicker cotton weave that lasts longer and takes prints well.
  - q: Do I need a gusset?
    a: A gusset gives the bag depth so it holds boxes and books. It also narrows the flat print area, so we plan your artwork around it.
  - q: Which handle length is best?
