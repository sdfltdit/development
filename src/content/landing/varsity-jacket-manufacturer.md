  - q: What is the difference between a varsity jacket and letterman jackets?
    a: They are the same style. Letterman jackets got their name from the school letter awarded to athletes. Today both names mean a wool body, contrast sleeves, snap front and striped rib trims.
  - q: How are chenille patches made?
    a: Chenille is made of looped yarn stitched onto felt, which gives the soft, raised texture. The felt is cut to shape, layered with a second felt border and sewn onto the jacket.
  - q: What are the sleeves made from?
    a: Most of our varsity jackets use PU faux leather or contrast melton wool sleeves. PU gives the classic shiny sleeve look and is easier to care for. We do not make real leather garments.
