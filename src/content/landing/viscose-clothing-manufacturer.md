  - q: Are you also a rayon clothing manufacturer?
    a: Yes. Rayon and viscose are the same fibre family. US labels use the generic name rayon, while UK and EU labels say viscose.
  - q: What is viscose challis?
    a: A light, plain-woven viscose with a soft, fluid drape. It is the most common choice for printed summer dresses, blouses and kurtas.
  - q: Does viscose fabric shrink?
    a: Yes, often more than cotton, and it is weaker when wet. We wash-test each lot and add the right allowance to the pattern before cutting.
  - q: Can I get custom viscose clothing in my own print?
    a: Yes. We screen print your artwork in our own factory, or you choose a ready mill print. Your colours are matched by lab dip before bulk.
