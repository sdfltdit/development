faq:
  - q: What is the minimum order for a garment washing service?
    a: Washing is part of a garment order, which starts at {{moq}} {{moqUnit}} per style. Different washes and sizes share the total, as agreed on your quote.
  - q: What is an enzyme wash?
    a: An enzyme wash softens fabric and fades colour gently using natural enzymes. It gives a clean, worn look with less damage than stones.
  - q: What is a stone wash?
    a: A stone wash tumbles garments with pumice stones. It fades denim unevenly and softens the fabric for a classic worn look.
  - q: How is an acid wash made?
    a: An acid wash uses stones soaked in bleach, tumbled dry with the garment. It gives sharp, marbled contrast on dark denim.
  - q: What makes a vintage wash look real?
    a: A vintage wash combines fading, hand sanding at stress points and soft worn hems. We build each effect to match your reference garment.
  - q: Will every piece look exactly the same?
    a: Close but not identical. Washing varies a little, so we approve a standard plus light and dark limits, and sort every piece against them.
  - q: Does washing change the size?
    a: Yes. We wash the sample first, then measure, and build the shrinkage into the pattern.
